PAGE TITLE
Character Prompt Spec 

PAGE DESCRIPTION
Nacho is the anthropomorphic nacho chip mascot used across the Nacho brand system. Nacho should feel like a vintage 1930s rubber-hose cartoon character adapted for a modern product brand: expressive, warm, slightly mischievous, and highly recognizable. The mascot is built from a puffy triangular tortilla chip body, black rubber-hose limbs, oversized white gloves, oversized white shoes, bold hand-inked outlines, halftone shading, toasted chip edges, and visible tortilla speckle texture.

SECTION 1: Overview

Use asset:

Content:
Nacho is the primary brand mascot. The character is a living tortilla chip with a single chip-shaped body that also functions as the head. Nacho’s personality should feel cheerful, helpful, confident, expressive, and a little playful. Nacho should never feel corporate, sterile, realistic, glossy, 3D, or overly polished.

Nacho works best when used as a guide, presenter, celebrator, explainer, or friendly visual accent inside Nacho product and marketing experiences.

Core personality traits:
- Helpful
- Energetic
- Playful
- Friendly
- Slightly mischievous
- Optimistic
- Expressive
- Vintage-inspired
- Simple and readable

Do not portray Nacho as:
- Mean
- Aggressive
- Realistic food
- A 3D mascot
- A taco
- A bag mascot
- A human child
- A flat corporate illustration
- A generic emoji-style character

SECTION 2: Character Proportions

Use assets:
- `Slice 18.png`
- `Slice 11.png`

Content:
Nacho’s body is a rounded triangular tortilla chip with irregular scalloped edges. The body is intentionally imperfect and organic. It should not be a perfect triangle.

Core proportion rules:
- Body shape: wide rounded triangle
- Approximate width-to-height ratio: 1.15:1
- Head and torso are one continuous chip shape
- Eyes sit in the upper-center area of the chip
- Nose sits between and slightly below the eyes
- Mouth sits below the nose, centered slightly low on the face
- Arms attach to the left and right sides of the chip
- Legs attach under the lower edge of the chip
- Shoes are large and rounded
- Gloves are oversized compared to the thin arms
- Feet and gloves help balance the simple chip body

Proportion guidance:
Nacho should read clearly even at small sizes. The eyes, mouth, gloves, and shoes should remain oversized and graphic. The body should stay compact and recognizable. Do not stretch Nacho into a tall, narrow triangle or flatten Nacho into a wide tortilla chip.

Use `Slice 18.png` to show the measured proportion reference. Use `Slice 11.png` as the neutral full-body reference.

SECTION 3: Shape Language

Use assets:
- `Slice 16.png`
- `Slice 20.png`

Content:
Nacho’s silhouette is built around soft, rounded, vintage cartoon shapes.

Body shape rules:
- Rounded triangular chip body
- Puffy, slightly dimensional form
- Uneven scalloped edge
- Toasted orange side edge visible on one side
- Organic curves instead of geometric precision
- Small bumps along the chip edge
- No sharp corners
- No perfectly straight sides

Edge rules:
The toasted edge gives Nacho depth and makes the mascot feel like a real chip without becoming realistic. The edge should appear as an orange-brown dimensional side plane, usually visible along the left or rear edge of the chip.

Use `Slice 16.png` and `Slice 20.png` to document the scalloped edge, toasted side treatment, and dimensional chip thickness.

SECTION 6: Texture System

Use assets:
- `Slice 19.png`
- `Slice 21.png`
- `Slice 23.png`
- `Slice 24.png`

Content:
Nacho’s surface texture is a key identity feature. The character should always include tortilla speckles, grain, halftone shading, and print-inspired imperfections.

Texture types:
1. Tortilla speckles
   Use `Slice 19.png`
   - Small brown dots scattered across the chip body
   - Irregular spacing
   - Varying dot size
   - Should feel baked into the chip
   - Not too dense or noisy

2. Toasted edge texture
   Use `Slice 20.png`
   - Orange-brown edge with darker speckles
   - Creates depth and baked character
   - Should appear on the chip’s side edge

3. Halftone shading
   Use `Slice 21.png`
   - Dot-based shading inspired by vintage print
   - Used in shadows and warm gradients
   - Should be subtle and controlled

4. Rubber-hose limb grain
   Use `Slice 22.png`
   - Black limbs include light grain and texture
   - Prevents arms and legs from feeling flat or digitally perfect

5. Ground shadow
   Use `Slice 23.png`
   - Stippled black/brown shadow under feet
   - Soft printed feel
   - Keeps character grounded without realistic cast shadows

6. Paper texture
   Use `Slice 24.png`
   - Subtle cream paper grain
   - Good for backgrounds, cards, and documentation examples

Texture rules:
- Texture should be visible but not distracting.
- Do not remove the tortilla speckles.
- Do not make the chip smooth or plastic.
- Do not use glossy gradients.
- Do not use realistic food photography texture.
- Do not over-texture the face so much that the expression becomes hard to read.
- Keep texture warm, printed, and vintage.

SECTION 11: Usage Guidelines

Content:
Use Nacho when the interface or communication benefits from warmth, playfulness, personality, or explanation.

Good use cases:
- Empty states
- Product onboarding
- Feature education
- Celebration moments
- Social media graphics
- Marketing illustrations
- Video player scenes
- Dashboard explainers
- Error states with light humor
- Setup checklists
- Product tips
- Campaign visuals

Avoid using Nacho:
- In dense data tables
- In serious legal or financial warnings
- In high-risk destructive actions
- As a replacement for clear UI copy
- Too many times on a single screen
- As a tiny decorative element where details are lost
- In styles that conflict with the mascot system

Placement rules:
- Give Nacho enough whitespace
- Do not crop off important limbs unless intentionally composed
- Avoid placing Nacho over visually busy backgrounds
- Use cream or warm neutral backgrounds when possible
- Keep contrast high enough for black outlines to remain crisp
- Do not place Nacho on colors that clash with the yellow chip body
